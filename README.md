A make.fun entry for **Laugh Track: The Crypto Trading Joke Contest** — one
line, under 200 characters, about crypto and trading life.

## The entry

> I sold my car for $8,000 to buy the dip. The dip is now worth less than the bus fare to the job I quit to trade full-time.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only) writes the line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), after validating the
competition gate: 1–200 characters, valid UTF-8, not only whitespace, a
single line, and no leading or trailing whitespace.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
```
